The NOV Flood 2026 Housing Verification map supports address checks for flood recovery in La Plata County.

Using the map

Use the map for housing verification and related flood recovery work. Use your own Google account and access the private spreadsheet only if you already have permission. Keep private addresses, case details, and screenshots that contain them within the authorized workflow.

Interpreting results

Spreadsheet Approved reflects a matching record. Map Approved reflects a matching address point within the Housing Grid or a yellow buffer zone. The result alone does not establish damage to an individual home or eligibility for assistance.

Spreadsheet records, address locations, and map layers can change. If a result is missing, ambiguous, or conflicts with other information, use the Call OEM contacts on the map.
