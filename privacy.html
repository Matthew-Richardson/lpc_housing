Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

This policy describes how the NOV Flood 2026 Housing Verification map uses information to check addresses for flood recovery.

Google account and spreadsheet data

When you connect Google Sheets, Google handles sign-in and asks you to authorize read-only spreadsheet access. That permission can cover spreadsheets your account can open. This app only requests the configured housing verification spreadsheet. Google enforces that spreadsheet’s existing sharing permissions.

The app reads the configured tab’s column headers and rows, which can include addresses and unit numbers, city, state, ZIP code, case numbers, damage or access reasons, source information, and other fields included on that tab. The app uses this information to find address matches, show case details, plot locations, and draw the yellow map zones. It does not change the spreadsheet.

Storage and retention

The app uses the Google access token in the browser to read the spreadsheet. It does not store the token or spreadsheet rows in browser local storage or a separate app database. Sheet data and the map layers derived from it are held in the open page and cleared when you disconnect, reload or close the page, or when the app’s access session expires. Your browser may remember spreadsheet column selections. This app does not delete records from the source spreadsheet.

Map services and sharing

GitHub Pages hosts the app. Google provides sign-in and spreadsheet access. La Plata County GIS and Esri provide map data and imagery. Map services receive requests for displayed geography. Address matching against the spreadsheet runs in the browser. If you choose Try Esri address lookup, the address in the search box is sent to Esri. The app does not upload spreadsheet rows to those map services.

The app does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Disconnect or remove access

Select Disconnect spreadsheet on the map to clear the current page’s sheet data. To remove the app’s Google authorization, manage it under your Google Account connections. Contact the spreadsheet owner about its sharing permissions or source records.

Contact

For questions about this map or its data, contact La Plata County Office of Emergency Management at 970-385-8700.
